# 🚀 Synergy Social Assistant (Chrome Extension)

Seamless Instagram Carousel Automation Companion for Synergy AI Studio.

---

## 📦 How to Install in 3 Easy Steps

1. Open **Google Chrome** and navigate to:
   ```text
   chrome://extensions/
   ```
2. Turn ON the toggle for **"Developer mode"** in the top right corner.
3. Click the **"Load unpacked"** button in the top left corner and select this folder (`synergy-extension`).

---

## ✅ How to Verify the Connection

1. Return to your **Synergy Web Studio** (`https://synergy.vercel.app` or `http://localhost:3000`).
2. Open **Content Studio** or **Templates** and click **`⚡ Auto-Post IG`**.
3. Click **"Verify Flow Integrity"**.
4. You will see a green checkmark confirming that the extension is connected and your Instagram session is authenticated.

---

## 🛡️ Security & Privacy
- **Zero Cloud Exposure**: Your Instagram session cookies never leave your computer.
- **100% Residential IP**: Actions run directly inside your daily Chrome browser, completely bypassing datacenter IP bans.
- **Anti-Flag Delays**: Automatically applies randomized delays to simulate human typing.
